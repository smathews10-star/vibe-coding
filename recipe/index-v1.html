Peanut Butter & Apple Sandwich

Serves
1
Time
5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread (whole wheat works well)
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter, smooth or crunchy
  • ½ crisp apple, such as Honeycrisp or Granny Smith
  • 1 teaspoon honey (optional)
  • A pinch of cinnamon (optional)

Steps

  1. Toast the bread lightly if you like a little crunch.
  2. Core the apple half and cut it into thin slices.
  3. Spread the peanut butter evenly over one side of each slice of bread.
  4. Layer the apple slices over one peanut-buttered slice.
  5. Drizzle with honey and sprinkle with cinnamon, if using.
  6. Top with the second slice, peanut butter side down, press gently, and cut in half.
