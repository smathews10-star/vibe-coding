Notes on the Peanut Butter & Apple Sandwich

The apple is what makes this sandwich. A crisp, firm variety like Honeycrisp or Granny Smith holds its crunch against the soft peanut butter, while softer apples can turn mealy. Tart apples balance the richness; sweeter ones pair nicely with a little honey.

Slice the apple as thinly as you can so the sandwich stays easy to bite. If you're packing it for later, toss the slices with a few drops of lemon juice first to keep them from browning, and spread the peanut butter right to the edges of the bread so it acts as a barrier against sogginess.

It's an easy recipe to change up. Try almond or sunflower seed butter instead of peanut butter, swap the bread for a tortilla and roll it up, or add a sprinkle of granola for extra crunch.
